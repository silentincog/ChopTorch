# Chop Torch privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026. The same text is in the app under Settings → Privacy policy.

## In short

Chop Torch does not collect, send or share any data. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone. There are no ads, analytics, accounts or tracking.

## What it uses on your phone, and why

- Motion sensor (accelerometer): to recognise your gesture.
- Proximity sensor: for pocket guard, to ignore chops in your pocket.
- Flashlight: to switch the torch.
- Vibration: to confirm the torch switched.
- Notifications: Android requires a visible notification for a service that runs in the background.
- Keep awake and start at boot: so the gesture works with the screen off and after a restart.
- Battery optimisation exemption: so Android doesn't stop the service.
- Root (optional): only when you tap Root keep-alive, or Import from Chop Torch 3.x.

## What it stores, and where

Your settings, your personal gesture model (short motion recordings of your gestures and of everyday movement, used to tell them apart) and the list of what it learned. Everything is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Start learning again and Forget everything learned delete parts of it.

## Calibration report

Copy report puts a text report on your clipboard, only when you tap it. It contains your phone model, sensor details and the force of your recorded gestures. You decide where to paste it.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app and in the repository. Questions: contact the author, incogcyberpunk.
